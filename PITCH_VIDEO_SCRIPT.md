## Notes for the reader

- Word count: ~115 words / 75 seconds — deliberately slow. Don't compress
  the pauses in Scene 4 to fit more words in; that silence around the route
  drawing IS the pitch. It's the one moment in the film built to be watched,
  not narrated over.
- Scene 6 should sound flatter than the rest — a factual statement, not a
  hype line.
- **Sync note**: in the rendered video, "SEE THE EVENT." / "CONNECT THE
  EVIDENCE." / "UNDERSTAND THE JOURNEY." reveal one line at a time starting
  at local frame 60 of Scene 8 (≈1:12.0), roughly half a second apart. If you
  want your voiceover to land word-for-word with each line appearing, speak
  those three lines starting right at 1:12.
- The rendered file (`pitch_video/out/sentinel-pitch.mp4`) has **no audio
  track** — it's silent by design, ready for you to lay voiceover over in
  your editor of choice, timed against the table above.
